2. Causal Research Design

Event-time design with explicit identification limits

Research question

10-K AI disclosure and filing-window reactions

Do firms' AI-related 10-K disclosures generate measurable post-filing capital-market reactions? The design tests filing-window associations using abnormal returns and abnormal volume.

Identification caution

Quasi-experimental event-time evidence

The pipeline supports causal empirical analysis, but any causal-effect interpretation requires successful market data, diagnostics, placebo checks, and careful limitations.

9. Robustness / Limitations

Current constraints

AI disclosure variables are text-based communication proxies, not direct AI adoption measures.
Topic labels are machine/topic-derived labels, not validated human coding.
Sector fields are approximate SEC SIC-derived NAICS enrichment, not fully verified firm-level NAICS classifications.
Market data were not collected successfully in this run, so no CAR, abnormal-volume, or regression estimates should be interpreted as empirical findings.
Successful execution requires a working external market-data source or a repository-local market index and firm price panel.
