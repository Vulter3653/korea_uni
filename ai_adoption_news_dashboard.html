Current dashboard scope

Official AI communication in annual filings

This dashboard treats 10-K reports as official corporate communication texts. It analyzes how Fortune 2025 Top 100 firms communicate AI through disclosure intensity, framing, specificity, communication mode, and application domains.

Data boundary

Repository-contained evidence only

The current dashboard uses only the data already present in the repository. Analyses requiring stock-price data are reserved for future extensions after those data are collected and audited.

2. 10-K as Official AI Communication Text

Why annual filings are useful communication data

Channel

Official Communication Channel

10-K is a formal, recurring, publicly accessible corporate communication text.

Framing

Strategic Framing

Firms do not merely mention AI; they define whether AI should be understood as opportunity, risk, governance, infrastructure, or application.

Audience

Institutional Audience

10-K reports are available to investors, regulators, analysts, journalists, and other external stakeholders.

Measurement

Text-as-Data Approach

AI-related sentences and windows are extracted, classified, and aggregated to firm-year communication measures.

3. AI Communication Dimensions

Descriptive measures derived from filing text

Intensity

AI Intensity

How frequently the firm mentions AI.

Opportunity

Opportunity Frame

AI as growth, innovation, productivity, efficiency, or competitive advantage.

Risk

Risk Frame

AI as legal, regulatory, cybersecurity, operational, ethical, or reputational risk.

Governance

Governance Frame

AI as an object of oversight, accountability, compliance, policy, or control.

Specificity

Specificity

Generic AI mention versus substantive disclosure tied to product, process, business unit, infrastructure, or mitigation.

Domain

Application Domain

Product/service, operation, customer/data, risk/security, workforce, infrastructure, or financial decision use.

4. Why Firms Communicate AI

Frame emphasis as corporate communication logic

Opportunity

Strategic Opportunity

AI as growth, innovation, productivity, or competitive advantage.

Risk

Risk Management

AI as exposure to legal, operational, security, or reputational risk.

Legitimacy

Governance Legitimacy

AI as an object of responsible management, oversight, and institutional control.

Infrastructure

Infrastructure Commitment

AI as long-term investment in data, cloud, models, platforms, or systems.

Customer

Customer / Market Orientation

AI as a tool for service quality, customer experience, personalization, or market adaptation.

Why / How split

Mechanism Logic

The dashboard separates why firms emphasize particular AI frames from how those frames are constructed in the 10-K text.

7. Measurement Design

From filing text to firm-year communication measures

1. Collect 10-K TextUse observed filing text already present in the repository sample.
2. Extract AI Sentences / WindowsIdentify AI-related text units for measurement.
3. Classify AI FramesAssign opportunity, risk, governance, infrastructure, and application themes.
4. Score Specificity and ModeDistinguish generic mentions from substantive disclosure and coding posture.
5. Aggregate to Firm-YearConvert extracted windows into firm-year communication measures.
6. Compare by Year and Firm CharacteristicsUse industry groups only after industry-code joins or manual grouping are validated.
RoleVariablesInterpretation
Analytical ConditionsYear, firm visibility, text coverage, and later industry/regulatory groups after code joinsConditions under which AI communication may vary
Communication OutcomesAI intensity, opportunity frame, risk frame, governance frame, specificity, application domainText-based measures of official AI communication
Validation ChecksTopic model consistency, dictionary checks, manual auditMeasurement reliability and interpretability

8. Limitations and Future Extensions

Current boundaries and later analysis paths

Current Limitations

External responses are not measured in the current repository.
Stock price data are not yet collected or joined.
Media response, analyst response, SNS response, Google Trends, and market response data are outside the current dashboard scope.
SIC/NAICS industry codes are not yet joined to the final manifest.
The current dashboard is descriptive and measurement-oriented.
Topic-model outputs are descriptive, not causal estimates.
Workflow hardening remains a later engineering task.

Future Extensions

Join SIC/NAICS or manually coded industry groups.
Add industry-level descriptive comparisons.
Validate frame classification through dictionary, topic model, and manual audit.
Collect stock-price data for publicly traded firms.
Build post-filing market outcome measures only after stock data are collected and audited.
Potential future outcomes: monthly return, abnormal return, volatility, trading volume.
Extend to media, analyst, or SNS response only if reliable data become available.
