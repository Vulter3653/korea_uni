4. AI Disclosure Metrics

Current metric basis in the final manifest

AI keyword count

Each successful 10-K text has ai_keyword_count and ai_keyword_per_10k_words from the collection pipeline.

Primary DV/IV candidate

AI disclosure intensity

The working measure is normalized AI keyword frequency per 10,000 words at the firm-year level.

Contextual disclosure coding
