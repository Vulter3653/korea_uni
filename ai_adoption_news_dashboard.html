2. Event-Study Research Design

Event-time design with explicit identification limits

Research question

10-K AI disclosure and filing-window reactions

Are firms' AI-related 10-K disclosures associated with measurable post-filing capital-market reactions? The design tests filing-window associations using abnormal returns and abnormal volume.

Identification caution

Quasi-experimental event-time evidence

The pipeline supports event-time empirical analysis, but it should not be read as standalone proof of an AI-specific causal effect.

7. Model-Free Evidence

What the event-window data show before regression controls

Returns

Small average CARs

Average CARs are slightly positive across the short filing-window specifications, ranging from 0.24% to 0.31%. The magnitudes are economically modest and should be interpreted as descriptive event-window patterns.

Trading attention

Elevated abnormal volume

Average abnormal volume is elevated across the [0,+1], [0,+3], and [0,+5] windows, with the [0,+1] window showing the largest increase. This is consistent with information processing around 10-K filing dates.

Interpretation

Limited AI-specific price pattern

The descriptive filing-window response does not by itself show that AI-related disclosure variables drive short-window returns. Regression evidence is needed to assess whether variation in AI communication explains market reactions.

Model-free limitation

Bundled 10-K information

Model-free evidence suggests that 10-K filings attract market attention, especially through abnormal trading volume. However, these descriptive patterns do not isolate AI-specific disclosure effects from the broader bundled information contained in annual reports.

11. Robustness / Limitations

Current constraints

AI disclosure variables are text-based communication proxies, not direct AI adoption measures.
Topic labels are machine/topic-derived labels, not validated human coding.
Sector fields are approximate SEC SIC-derived NAICS enrichment, not fully verified firm-level NAICS classifications.
Short-window market reactions do not systematically correlate with AI strategic disclosure intensity in this sample.
A causal interpretation requires accounting for the bundled information nature of 10-K filings.
