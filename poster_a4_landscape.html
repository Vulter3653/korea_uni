Corporate AI Communication and Capital Market Response

A 10-K filing event study of Fortune 2025 Top 100 firms. The study examines whether AI-related language in annual reports is associated with stock-market and trading-volume reactions around the filing date.

Plain-language question: When a firm talks more about AI in its annual report, does the market react differently around the day that report becomes public?
Underlying Mechanism
Public 10-K disclosureAI-related language becomes observable to investors.
→
Investor attentionInvestors process new annual-report information.
→
Trading responseAttention appears first as abnormal volume.
→
Price adjustmentCAR changes only if the AI signal is value-relevant and separable.
Mechanism logic: AI communication can act as a signal of innovation opportunity or risk exposure. Because 10-K reports bundle many financial and strategic disclosures, the design expects clearer evidence in trading attention than in AI-specific short-window returns.

01 · Terms

Key Terms in Plain Language

10-K annual reportA required yearly report filed by U.S. public companies. It contains business, risk, and financial information.
Event dateThe day the 10-K report is filed and becomes observable to the market.
AI communication proxyA text-based measure of how much and how a firm discusses AI. It is not a direct measure of actual AI adoption.
Market reactionWhether stock returns or trading volume move unusually around the filing date.
02 · Data

Design Summary

  • Sample: Fortune 2025 Top 100 firms
  • Event window: days around 10-K filing
  • Estimation window: [-250, -30] trading days
  • Benchmark: US500 first, then SPY / ^GSPC / IVV / VOO where available
  • Validation status: FINAL VERDICT PASS
03 · Formula

How Market Reaction Is Calculated

Abnormal Return

ARi,t = Ri,t − (αi + βiRm,t)

Meaning: actual firm return minus the return expected from general market movement.

Cumulative Abnormal Return

CARi,[a,b] = Σt=ab ARi,t

Meaning: abnormal returns added across the event window, such as [0,+1] or [0,+5].

04 · IV

Independent Variables

  • AI_Related_Disclosure_Intensity: how intensively the report discusses AI.
  • AI_RiskRelated_Topic_Share: how much AI discussion appears in risk-related text.
  • AI_Risk_Orientation_Proxy: whether AI discussion is framed more toward risk orientation.
  • Boundary: text proxies, not direct AI adoption.
05 · Model

Regression Model

Yi,t = β0 + β1AICommi,t + γXi,t + μi + λt + εi,t

Y is CAR or abnormal volume. β1 tests whether AI-related 10-K communication is associated with market reaction.

μi = firm fixed effectsControls for stable differences across firms.
λt = year fixed effectsControls for year-level market or reporting conditions.
Clustered standard errorsA conservative way to calculate uncertainty when observations from the same firm may be related.

08 · Result

Final Takeaway

10-K filings appear to attract market attention, especially through elevated abnormal trading volume.

However, AI-specific disclosure variation does not systematically explain short-window abnormal returns in the current sample.

Interpretation limit: this is event-window association evidence, not standalone proof of an AI-specific causal effect.
