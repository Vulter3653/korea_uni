Question

Research Question

Do firms' AI-related 10-K disclosures relate to abnormal capital-market reactions around the 10-K filing date?

Design

Event-Study Design

  • Sample: Fortune 2025 Top 100 firms
  • Event date: 10-K filing_date
  • Estimation window: [-250, -30] trading days
  • CAR windows: [-1,+1], [0,+1], [0,+3], [0,+5]
  • Abnormal volume windows: [0,+1], [0,+3], [0,+5]
  • Benchmark: US500 first, then SPY / ^GSPC / IVV / VOO where available

Takeaway

Takeaway

10-K filings appear to attract market attention, especially through abnormal trading volume. However, AI-specific disclosure variation does not systematically explain short-window abnormal returns in the current design.

This is event-window association evidence, not standalone proof of an AI-specific causal effect.
